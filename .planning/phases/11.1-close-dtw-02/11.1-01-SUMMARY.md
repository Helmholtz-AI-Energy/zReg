## Decisions Made

- `callable(dist)` check uses `continue` to skip the rest of the loop body, including the SWD downsampling guard, so callable metrics are exempt from the `downsample_method is None` RuntimeError
- DistanceMetric annotation uses a union (`str | DistanceMetric`) rather than replacing `str` entirely to maintain backward compatibility with all existing callers
- conftest.py fixed with open3d-before-torch import guard to resolve macOS ARM libomp crash
