<objective>
Implement the hand-rolled PointNet++ joint-cloud model: `SetAbstraction` (FPS → ball-query →
per-group relative-position+feature MLP → max-pool), `FeaturePropagation` (3-NN inverse-distance
interpolation + skip connection + MLP), and `PointNet2LabelTransfer` composing 2 SA + 2 FP + a
per-point classification head over the concatenated source+target cloud.

Purpose: PointNet++ is one of the two learned label-transfer architectures locked in 45-DESIGN.md.
The joint-cloud conditioning (one-hot label on source rows, unknown-flag bit on target rows) is the
mechanism by which labeled-source signal propagates to unlabeled-target points through local
geometric neighborhoods.
Output: `src/zreg/models/pointnet2.py`, `tests/test_zreg_models_pointnet2.py`.
</objective>
