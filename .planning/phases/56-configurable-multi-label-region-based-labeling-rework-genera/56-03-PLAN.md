<objective>
Add `EvalConfig.label_generation` (a `LabelGenerationConfig` pydantic model wrapping `list[LabelSpec]`/`n_labels`, `mode`, and `seed`) so scenario YAML can declare label generation declaratively (D-13), and complete the `n_classes` -> `n_labels` rename cascade (D-04) through `DataFactory.generate_training_triple`/`generate_training_set` and every internal call site that forwards into them, plus `eval/runners/optimizer.py`'s sanity-tier call to `generate_labels`.

</objective>
